01 / OVERVIEW

The Orchadium under siege

The Orchadium has long supplied the village with abundant harvests. Now mushroom creatures are advancing from the surrounding forests, threatening the crops and the barn.

Players form a team of gardeners. They coordinate shared plots, plant and harvest crops, then carry the produce to giant towers as ammunition. Keeping the garden productive and the defenses supplied becomes a constant, cooperative task.

Later waves unlock new plants. Some take longer or need more plots, but give towers special projectile effects. Clear every wave and defeat the bosses to win; let too many enemies reach the base and the Orchadium falls.

03 / SYSTEMS

Farming is the defense

Towers cannot generate their own ammunition. Every shot starts with a crop that players have planted, grown, harvested, and delivered.

Protect the Orchadium

WIN CONDITION

Defeat the bosses

Clear every boss on the map.

LOSE CONDITION

Base health reaches zero

Too many Mushrooms passing the towers bring down the garden.

Core player actions

  • Plant: fill empty plots with seeds.
  • Harvest: gather mature crops.
  • Load: carry crops to towers as ammunition.
  • Expand: open new land for resources and influence enemy routes.

Core challenges

  • Allocate resources between defense supplies and expansion.
  • Respond to different enemy waves with suitable crop ammunition and tower placement.
  • Coordinate planting, harvesting, and delivery as a team.
